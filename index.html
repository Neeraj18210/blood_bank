WHO WE ARE

A student-built blood bank management system for a real community need

Neeraj Blood Bank was created to make blood availability easier to manage during emergencies.

Our mission

To make safe blood reachable to every patient, irrespective of their ability to pay.

The management system digitises donor records, stock levels, blood requests and issue information, reducing dependence on paper records.

Become a donor

Register as a voluntary donor and help someone in need.

Donor registration

Fields marked with * are required.

You can donate if

  • ✓ Age between 18 and 65 years
  • ✓ Body weight above 50 kg
  • ✓ Haemoglobin of 12.5 g/dL or more
  • ✓ At least 90 days since your last donation

Please wait if

  • ✕ Fever, cold or infection recently
  • ✕ Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • ✕ Recent tattoo or piercing
  • ✕ Certain uncontrolled medical conditions

Before you come

Eat a proper meal, drink enough water, sleep well the night before and carry a government photo ID.
